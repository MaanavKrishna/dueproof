## Style

The notice may be a scan or photo with OCR errors, may mix English with an
Indian language, or may be an unusual format. Quote exactly what the page
text says, including OCR errors, so the quote can be found. When the same
duty is written in two languages, report it once per language, each with its
own quote. Read every page before answering.

Questions

Is the free version limited?

No. There is no feature, document or user limit. The paid plans sell running it and supporting it, not features.

Will features move from free to paid?

Nothing that affects whether a finding is correct or provable will ever be paid-only. Paid work is hosting, connectors to proprietary systems and support.

Can we use it commercially?

Yes. Apache 2.0 allows commercial use, modification and distribution. Keep the licence and notice files.

Does it give tax or legal advice?

No. It reads documents and tracks dates. Decisions stay with you and your advisers.

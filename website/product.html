What Dueproof does with a notice.

It reads it, finds what you owe and by when, files it with the right desk, and shows you the sentence behind every answer. Then it keeps track until the work is done.

Click a finding. See where it came from.

This is how every finding behaves in Dueproof. Try it on this notice.

OFFICE OF THE ASSISTANT COMMISSIONER OF STATE TAX
Commercial Taxes Department, Northline Division
Reference No: ZD2910260012345Date: 20 October 2026

Subject: Notice under Section 61 for intimating discrepancies in the return (Form GST ASMT-10)

On scrutiny of the returns filed for the above period, discrepancies were noticed in the input tax credit claimed.

You are hereby directed to explain the discrepancies and pay the tax of Rs. 4,52,300 with interest within 30 days of receipt of this notice.

A personal hearing is fixed on 18 November 2026 at 11:00 AM before the undersigned.

Failure to reply will attract proceedings under Section 73(1) of the Act.

Sd/- Assistant Commissioner of State Tax

What it finds

  • Obligations: sentences that direct someone to act, with a severity.
  • Due dates: only where the sentence frames a date as a deadline, so issue dates and tax periods stay out.
  • From receipt: "within 30 days of receipt" counts from when you received it. Correct the received date and those deadlines move; nothing else does.
  • Hearings: dated appointments, even with no deadline word.
  • The notice itself: type, reference, issuing office, amount demanded, provisions cited.

Workflow

Each obligation has an owner, a state (open, done, not a duty), a due date and desk you can correct, and a note. Corrections never overwrite what was found, and the history of every change cannot be edited, even from the database.

The deadline line shows open obligations in date order for your desk, with a badge for what falls due this week.

Reminders

A weekday email to each person with their open obligations due soon and everything overdue: what is assigned to them, plus unassigned items on their desk. It runs from cron or a systemd timer on your server.

Search, export, audit

  • Search every page of every document, in any script; hits open with the words outlined.
  • Export the register as a spreadsheet, or a printable evidence report with each obligation above a crop of its page. Print it to PDF for a review or an inspection.
  • An append-only audit trail: sign-ins, uploads, views, edits, exports.
  • The same document arriving twice, as a PDF and as a photo of its printout, is recognised and linked.

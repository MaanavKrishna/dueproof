22 Indian languages, read on your machine.

English is always on. Add any of India's scheduled languages and Dueproof reads their notices with the same rules, through a translation model that runs locally. The words outlined on the page are always the original.

No vocabulary to write

Each sentence in another language is translated to English before it is analysed, and the rules read the original and the translation together. A Tamil circular yields dated, routed obligations although nobody wrote a word of Tamil vocabulary.

Doubt is shown, never hidden

Translations are labelled. One that seems to have dropped a number or a known term is flagged so a reader checks the original. Dueproof never rewrites a translation to "fix" it.

Turn languages on

DUEPROOF_LANGUAGES=hi,ta,bn dueproof serve

Languages that share a script

Hindi, Marathi, Nepali and others share Devanagari; Bengali and Assamese share a script, as do Urdu, Sindhi and Kashmiri. Among the languages you enable, the first one listed reads that script.
